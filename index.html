Research & Work

⭐

Stellar Parameters & Extinction at Scale

Full stellar parameter and dust-extinction posteriors for hundreds of millions of stars (StarHorse / SHBoost), chrono-chemical mapping of the Milky Way disc, bulge and bar with Gaia DR3, APOGEE and 4MOST, and the 4MIDABLE survey design for 4MOST.

  • Gaia

  • Milky Way

Galaxy Formation & Hestia

High-resolution cosmological simulations of the Local Group and early galaxy assembly — from the first thin discs at z > 4 to AGN feedback in galaxy evolution (MareNostrum, Hestia).

  • simulation

Scientific Astrophysics
