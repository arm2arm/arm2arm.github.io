Research & Work

SH26 — Galactic Bulge Catalogue

A 400+ million-star spectroscopic catalogue of the Milky Way bulge and disc. I own the end-to-end data pipeline: schema design, float32 migration, a full-catalogue Dask/Parquet join (210 GB), and the publication-quality figure suite.

⭐

StarHorse & AVFIX

StarHorse fits full stellar parameter + extinction posteriors (GMM3) for hundreds of millions of stars. I discovered a systematic dust-extinction prior bug, derived the true affected region, and designed the 940-tile / 69.9M-star rerun — a fault-tolerant, journal-resuming v5 runner driving a 400+ node SLURM cluster to completion.

  • StarHorse
  • SLURM
  • fault-tolerance
  • extinction
